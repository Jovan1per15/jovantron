Hi, I'm Jordan.

I'm a brand and visual designer based in Chicago, working with food, culture, and consumer companies who need a visual identity that can stretch across packaging, environments, and campaigns without losing its shape.

My process starts with the strategy — what a brand needs to say and to whom — before a single mark gets drawn. I've led identity work for startups launching their first product and for established brands going through a full repositioning.
